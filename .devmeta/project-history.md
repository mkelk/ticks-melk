## 2026-11-12 — Increment 01-kyl "Lean engine", E1 pjz: Lean brief and two-tier verification
The engine now names a **tick gate** (per tick, in the worktree: typecheck, reached tests, build) and a **wave gate** (per wave or chain integration, by the integrator: full suite, slow suites, specs, shared outputs regenerated once). The implementer prompt is shaped as criteria, gates, boundaries and report, with a three-outcome line per mandated check, which E2's sunset step will tally. A planted fault is required only where the logic can be wrong. One frontier plan, one strong warm-chain of five ticks (one cold start), and a frontier review with six findings, fixed as a 7.5k-token warm turn. The host's load (~40–54) made the local Pi and Go suites flaky on the baseline too; a base/HEAD/base A/B showed parity, and CI on PR #5 was green. Retro: `increments/increment-01-kyl/ia-cycles/lean-brief-and-two-tier-verification.md`.

## 2026-11-12 — Increment 01-kyl "Lean engine", E2 z5t: Lean dispatch and context
The engine's default dispatch is now **2-3 parallel warm-chains** per epic: file-disjoint, grouped by subsystem and tier, balanced against the longest tick, with the rate window as a width cap. Implementers stay warm until the final review and fix its findings as **warm turns**. The learnings became a ≤4 KB always-read **head** (`.tick/learnings.md`) plus a looked-up **body** (`.tick/learnings-ref.md`), and `tk status`/`tk close` warn per file. A **steering-sunset** retro step retires per-tick checks that catch nothing for two epics; its first run demoted nothing and surfaced two gaps. The epic ran on its own rules: three chains, three cold starts for eleven ticks, seventeen review findings fixed warm in under three minutes each. This repo's learnings were migrated to head plus body. Full retro: `.devmeta/increments/increment-01-kyl/ia-cycles/lean-dispatch-and-context.md`.
